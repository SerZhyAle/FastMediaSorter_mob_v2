## 🌟 Desktop Launcher Capabilities

FastMedia Launcher transforms any Android device into an organized, media-focused desktop. Instead of a cluttered app grid, you get a clean desktop displaying your custom folder shortcuts, live clock, weather updates, and media player controls.

### 🚗 In-Car Android Head Units & Tablets
- **Touch-Friendly Dashboard:** Large, high-contrast shortcut tiles for quick access to your music, offline playlists, and network drives while driving.
- **Steering Wheel Button Integration:** Seamless track skipping and volume adjustment via car steering wheel controls and physical knobs.
- **Sticky Streams Radio Bar:** Listen to internet radio stations directly on your head unit dashboard with continuous ICY track info display.
- **Auto-Start on Ignition:** Launches directly into your custom desktop as soon as the car ignition powers on the Android stereo.

### 🖼️ Dedicated Photo Frame Tablets
- **Always-On Digital Frame:** Turn old budget tablets into interactive photo frames streaming photos from home NAS (SMB) or cloud storage.
- **Background Music Support:** Enjoy photo slideshows accompanied by your favorite background music tracks.
- **Clock & Weather Overlay:** Displays ambient time, date, and local weather forecasts alongside photo slideshows.

### 📺 Android TV Boxes & Media Centers
- **Full D-Pad & Remote Control Support:** Navigate folders and media using standard TV remotes, Bluetooth controllers, or air-mice.
- **Color-Coded Remote Shortcuts:** Red, Green, Yellow, and Blue remote buttons map directly to file operations (Delete, Copy, Move, Rename).
- **Big-Screen Cinema Mode:** Stream 4K movies and series directly from home servers without local storage limits.

## 📸 Desktop Layout & Structure

FastMedia Launcher gives you full control over your device's home screen layout:

```
.---------------------------------------------------------.
|  [ 12:45 PM | ☀️ 22°C ]              [ ⚙️ Settings ]    |
|---------------------------------------------------------|
|  .-------------.   .-------------.   .---------------.  |
| | 📁 Dash Cam   | | 🎵 Car Music| | 🖼️ Photo Frame|  |
| | (Local SD)    | | (NAS SMB)   | | (Cloud Sync)  |  |
|  '-------------'   '-------------'   '---------------'  |
|---------------------------------------------------------|
| [ 📻 Internet Radio ]  [ 📱 App Drawer ]  [ 🧹 Sort ]   |
'---------------------------------------------------------'
```

- **Custom Folder Shortcuts:** One-tap shortcuts to local folders, USB drives, NAS shares, or cloud storage.
- **Built-in Application Drawer:** Quickly open any installed Android application directly from the launcher taskbar.
- **Desktop Locking:** Lock desktop editing to prevent accidental changes during daily driving or photo frame operation.
- **Live Camera Background:** Set the desktop wallpaper to a live frame from one of the device's cameras. The entry appears in the wallpaper list only on devices that have a camera; choosing it asks for the camera permission and then lets you pick which lens to use. The stream stops the moment the desktop leaves the screen, so nothing runs in the background, and a device without a camera or with the permission refused keeps the branded wallpaper instead of a black screen.
- **Stream Window Tile:** Put one internet channel on the desktop and pick it when you add the tile. A radio channel gets a small tile with its name, its icon and a play button. A video channel gets a larger tile with the picture playing inside it; tap to stop, and the tile keeps the last frame instead of going blank. Nothing starts playing until you press play.
