# Clouds: a shared box for your devices

A cloud account is the simplest way to share media between your devices when they are never on the same network: one device puts files in, another opens them. **It works today**, in every edition except Lite and FOSS - see [Editions](editions.html).

## Which clouds

- **Google Drive** - signs in with your Google account.
- **Dropbox** - signs in through Dropbox.
- **OneDrive** - signs in with your Microsoft account.

Each cloud appears in FastMediaSorter as a resource next to the local folders and network folders, so copying, moving and sorting work the same way across all of them.

## What else Google Drive carries

- **Settings backup** - one device backs up the app settings to Drive, and another device signed in to the same account restores them.
- **App data** - settings, favorites, pinned streams and resources travel between your devices as one file per kind in your Drive.
- **The phone's SFTP address** - with **Share access through Google Drive** switched on, Drive keeps the current address of the phone's SFTP server, so your other devices find it after a network change. See [Exchange server](exchange-server.html#what-works-today).

## When a cloud is the wrong choice

A cloud stores a copy of your files on someone else's servers and costs upload time for large videos. To open media where it already lives - on a PC or a NAS at home - use the [home network](home-network.html) instead.
