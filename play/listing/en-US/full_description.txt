Fast Media Sorter is an all-in-one file manager and media player for Android. Browse, sort, view and play your photos, videos and music from local storage, network shares and cloud - in one app, without installing a separate viewer for every format or source.

One app instead of ten:

• File manager across sources - local folders, SMB/CIFS, FTP, SFTP, Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox, side by side.
• Photo & image viewer - rotate, color filters, crop, draw annotations, OCR text extraction and AR-style translation overlay, save a compressed copy.
• Video player on ExoPlayer (Media3) - Picture-in-Picture, sleep timer, per-file resume, 3D/stereo and 360° detection.
• Music player - background playback, lyrics search, album art, visualizers and Chromecast output.
• Live TV & radio - thousands of internet channels with search, topic filters, pinned favorites, station artwork and now-playing information.
• Built-in camera - device-matched lenses, zoom range and photo sizes, shooting straight into the folder you pick.
• Slideshow - background music, per-resource interval, play-video-to-end, configurable transitions.
• PDF & EPUB reader - text-to-speech, themes, search and in-place translation.
• Text editor - charset auto-detection, Markdown rendering, syntax highlight.

Built to move files fast:

• Copy, move, rename and delete across protocols, including cross-cloud transfers with progress and ETA.
• Scheduled operations - set up a transfer between two folders once and let it run on its own.
• Share a network folder as a QR code, so another device joins it by scanning.
• Trash with a 5-minute recovery window.
• Thumbnails, advanced sort and filters, multi-select and drag-to-reorder.

For every screen and input:

• Full keyboard, TV remote / D-pad and gamepad navigation.
• Home-screen widgets - resource launch, continue reading, random music, camera photos.
• Quick Settings tile for background audio. Search across every settings entry.

Why the app asks for access to all files:

Sorting is what this app is for - you open a source folder, look at each file and copy or move it into a destination folder somewhere else on the device. That workflow has to reach the folders you choose, not only the app's own private storage, which is why the all-files permission is requested. Nothing leaves your device because of it: there is no account and no telemetry.

Free, open and private - the app source code is published under Apache License 2.0, and local, SMB, FTP and SFTP browsing plus the image, video, audio, PDF and EPUB viewers all work fully offline, without any account.
