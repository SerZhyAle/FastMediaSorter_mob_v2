---
name: third-party-branding-not-a-blocker
description: Owner stance - third-party favicons/logos shown next to that producer's own content are NOT a licensing blocker; don't gate features on it
type: feedback
---

Do not raise third-party brand assets (favicons, channel logos, station icons) as a licensing/IP blocker when they are shown next to that same producer's own content. Do not gate a feature, restrict it to `noLegal`, or ask the owner to justify it on those grounds.

**Why:** Owner stated directly (2026-06-25, S0668 favicon sprite-map) "лицензии.. не волнует - я показываю favicon рядом с контентом того же производителя.. не собираюсь ничего доказывать тебе - это не блокер." He considers showing a producer's mark alongside the producer's own stream/content self-evidently fine and was mildly annoyed at being asked to defend it.

**How to apply:** When a feature surfaces a channel/site/producer's favicon or logo adjacent to that producer's content, treat licensing as resolved - keep the feature at the broadest legal flavor (see [[push-features-to-lowest-flavor]]), do not add a legal-scope §6 question, do not default to noLegal. This is narrower than wholesale redistribution of third-party assets unrelated to their content - if a future case bundles brand assets detached from the producer's own content, that may still warrant a flag.
