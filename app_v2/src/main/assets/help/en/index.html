Terms

All apps
All apps is the launcher's full list of the apps installed on the device, opened from the launcher menu.
All Documents
All Documents is a virtual resource that lists every document found on this device, such as PDF, EPUB, text and Office files, in one place.
All Files
All Files is a ready-made resource that shows every kind of file on the device, not only media, and it works once All Files mode is allowed in Settings.
All Images
All Images is a virtual resource that lists every picture found on this device, such as JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP files, in one place.
All Music
All Music is a virtual resource that lists every audio file found on this device, such as MP3 or FLAC tracks, in one place.
All Videos
All Videos is a virtual resource that lists every video file found on this device in one place.
app
An app is any application installed on the Android device, such as a browser or a messenger, that the launcher can list and start.
audio player
The audio player is the player screen when it plays music or other audio, with the cover picture, the track controls and the queue.
badge
A badge is a small label drawn on top of a thumbnail or row, such as GIF, VR or Pinned, that tells you something about the item at a glance.
Calculator
Calculator is a built-in program for everyday sums that you switch on in Settings and open from the programs menu, a widget or the watch.
camera
The camera is the app's built-in way to take photos and record videos and save them straight into a resource or folder you choose.
Camera OCR
Camera OCR is a program that takes a photo, finds the text in it and shows a translation next to the original words.
Camera Photos
Camera Photos is a virtual resource that shows the photos and videos taken by this device's camera.
car head unit
A car head unit is the Android screen built into the dashboard of a car, where the app can play media with large, easy buttons.
catalog
The catalog is the built-in list of internet radio and TV channels that the app downloads so you can add them to Streams without typing any web addresses.
channel
A channel is one saved radio, TV or video station in the Streams list, with its own name, logo and web address.
Chromecast
Chromecast is the technology from Google for sending a video, photo or song from the app to a TV so it plays there instead of on the phone.
cloud provider
A cloud provider is the online service whose storage you connect to the app: Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
cloud storage
Cloud storage is online space from a service such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, where your files are kept on the internet instead of on a device.
color theme
The color theme sets the overall look of the app, such as light, dark or automatic.
command bar
The command bar is the row of buttons across the top of the resource list, such as Add, Filter, Refresh, Settings and Start Player; when the screen is too narrow it first hides the button labels and then moves the buttons that still do not fit into the three-dots menu.
command panel
The command panel is the set of buttons in the player for acting on the current file, such as copy, move and delete.
D-pad
The D-pad is the four-arrow pad with a center button on a TV remote, game controller or keyboard that moves the selection around the screen.
desktop
The desktop is the launcher's main page, a grid where you place app icons, resources, channels, gadgets and sections, with separate layouts for the upright and the sideways screen.
destination
A destination is a resource you have marked for Quick Sort, so that one tap can copy or move the current file into it.
device profile
A device profile is a preset for the kind of device you use, such as a personal smartphone, a TV box, a car head unit or a photo frame, that sets sensible defaults for the whole app.
drawing editor
The drawing editor is the screen where you draw or write by hand on a photo, a screenshot or a blank page and save the result as a picture.
Dropbox
Dropbox is a cloud storage service that the app can open as a resource after you sign in to your Dropbox account.
edge gesture
An edge gesture is a short swipe that starts on a band at the left or right edge of the screen and runs a chosen action, such as a screenshot, over any app.
edition
An edition is one of the separately published versions of Fast Media Sorter & Organizer, each made for a certain kind of device or store and with its own set of features.
extension
An extension is an optional part of the app, such as text recognition or an extra audio decoder, that you download only when you want it and can delete again to free space.
Fast Media Sorter & Sharing for Windows
Fast Media Sorter & Sharing for Windows is a separate program for Windows PCs that shares chosen PC folders with the phone app in a couple of clicks, paired by scanning a QR code.
Favorites
Favorites is your own list of the files and channels you marked with a star (a heart on the watch), so you can open them again quickly from one place.
file
A file is a single item stored in a folder, such as one photo, song, video or document.
file browser
The file browser is the screen that opens when you open a resource and shows its files and subfolders so you can view, select, copy, move or delete them.
folder
A folder is a place on a disk, memory card or server where files are kept, and it exists whether or not the app knows about it.
FOSS edition
The FOSS edition is the open-source edition published in F-Droid, built without any closed-source parts, so it has no cloud storage, Streams, OCR, translation, Chromecast or watch companion.
FTP
FTP is an older, unencrypted way to reach folders on a server, still used by many routers and home servers.
gadget
A gadget is a small live block on the launcher desktop, such as a clock, weather, compass, battery or map, that shows fresh information and reacts to taps.
Google Drive
Google Drive is the cloud storage from Google, which the app can open as a resource after you sign in with your Google account.
grid view
Grid view shows files, resources or channels as picture tiles arranged in rows and columns.
home screen
The home screen is the Android start screen you see after unlocking the device, run by whichever launcher app is set as home.
image viewer
The image viewer is the player screen when it shows a photo or picture, where you can zoom, rotate, crop or start a slideshow.
launcher
The launcher is the part of Fast Media Sorter & Organizer that can replace the device's home screen with its own desktop, taskbar and app list, or show that desktop inside the app.
Legacy edition
The Legacy edition is an edition for older Android devices, starting from Android 6, with most Standard features but without the launcher and the watch companion.
list view
List view shows files, resources or channels one per line, with the name and details next to a small picture.
Lite edition
The Lite edition is a lighter edition that plays local photos, videos and music only, without network folders, cloud storage, documents or Streams.
Live Broadcast
Live Broadcast is a program that sends what the microphone of your phone, and optionally its camera, picks up live over the local network so that other devices can listen or watch.
local folder
A local folder is a folder stored on this device itself or on its memory card, as opposed to one on another computer or online.
media file
A media file is a file the app can show or play, such as a photo, GIF, video, audio track or document.
network folder
A network folder is a shared folder on another computer, a NAS or a router on your home or office network that the app opens over SMB.
Network Monitor
Network Monitor is a program that shows your connection, signal strength, addresses and speed so you can see why the network is slow or down.
network resource
A network resource is a resource that points to a folder on another computer or server over the network, using SMB, FTP or SFTP.
noLegal edition
The noLegal edition is a sideload-only edition, installed from an APK file outside the app stores, that adds what stores do not allow, such as the immersive VR player and full storage access.
Now Playing
Now Playing is the view that shows the track playing right now, with its cover and controls, and leads back to the full audio player.
OCR
OCR (text recognition) reads the printed or written words in a photo or document page so you can copy, save or translate them.
OneDrive
OneDrive is the cloud storage from Microsoft, which the app can open as a resource after you sign in with a Microsoft account.
panel
A panel is a strip of buttons or items along one edge of a screen that can usually be collapsed or hidden.
permission
A permission is an access right that Android lets you give to or take back from an app, such as reading your photos, using the camera or drawing over other apps; Fast Media Sorter & Organizer asks only for the permissions its features need and lists them all on one screen.
phone
The phone is the Android phone or tablet that runs the main Fast Media Sorter & Organizer app and that the watch connects to.
Photos edition
The Photos edition is an edition for pictures only, with network folders and cloud storage but without video, audio, documents or Streams.
PIN
A PIN is a code of 4 to 6 digits you can set on a resource so that nobody can open it without typing the code.
player
The player is the full-screen screen that shows one file at a time, whether it is a photo, a GIF, a video or an audio track, and lets you move to the next or previous file.
playlist
A playlist is a list of tracks or channels that play one after another, such as an M3U file you import into Streams or the Playlist gadget on the launcher desktop.
program
A program is one of the extra tools built into Fast Media Sorter & Organizer, such as Calculator, Stopwatch, Network Monitor or the flashlights, that you switch on in Settings and open from the programs menu.
programs panel
The programs panel is an optional bar above the resource list that gives one-tap access to your programs.
queue
The queue is the list of tracks lined up to play next in the audio player.
Quick Sort
Quick Sort is the feature that lets you copy or move the file you are viewing into one of your numbered destinations with a single tap.
quick-access panel
The quick-access panel is a pop-up panel of your chosen apps and shortcuts that you can open from the launcher or with a gesture at the screen edge.
reader
The reader is the screen that opens documents and books, such as PDF, EPUB and text files, page by page.
resource
A resource is an entry you add to the app that points to a place with your files, such as a folder on this device, a network folder, a cloud folder, a stream or a watch, and it appears as one item in the resource list.
resource list
The resource list is the first screen of the app, where your resources are listed as rows or tiles and where you add, open and manage them.
resource profile
A resource profile is a preset you pick for one resource, such as Audio Library, Video Library or Photo Storage, that tunes which files it shows and how it opens them.
resource-type tabs
The resource-type tabs are the strip of tabs under the command bar in the resource list - ALL, Local, SMB, S/FTP, Cloud and Favorites - that show only the resources of one kind.
scheduled operation
A scheduled operation is a file job, such as copying or moving files from one resource to another, that the app runs by itself on the schedule you set.
screen recording
Screen recording is a program that records everything shown on the screen, with sound from the microphone, into a video file until you stop it.
screenshot
A screenshot is a still picture of everything shown on the screen at one moment, saved as an image file.
section
A section is a named group on the launcher desktop, such as Gadgets, Resources or Apps, that keeps related icons together under one heading.
Settings
Settings is the screen where you change how the app looks and behaves and switch optional programs and features on or off.
SFTP
SFTP is an encrypted way to reach folders on a server or computer by its address, a user name and a password or key.
shortcut
A shortcut is an icon you place on the home screen or the launcher desktop that opens one resource, channel, program or contact directly.
slideshow
A slideshow shows the files of a folder one after another by itself, moving to the next photo after the number of seconds you set.
SMB
SMB is the common way Windows PCs, NAS boxes and routers share folders over a home or office network, and the app uses it to open network folders.
Standard edition
The Standard edition is the recommended full edition for phones, tablets, TVs and car head units.
startup splash
The startup splash is the screen with the Fast Media Sorter & Organizer logo and name that you see for the moment the app takes to start; it disappears as soon as the app is ready.
stream
A stream is a live internet radio, TV or video feed that the app plays from a web address instead of from a saved file.
Streams
Streams is the screen that lists your internet radio, TV and video channels, where you add, pin, edit and play them.
streams panel
The streams panel is an optional bar above the resource list that shows your pinned channels for one-tap playback.
taskbar
The taskbar is the launcher's bar at the top or bottom of the screen with the Start button, pinned apps, recently used apps, the clock and status icons.
text note
A text note is a plain text file you write in the app and save into the current folder or resource.
three-dots menu
The three-dots menu is the menu that opens from the button with three vertical dots and holds the less common actions for an item or a screen.
touch zones
Touch zones are invisible areas of the full-screen player, laid out as a grid of nine, where a tap does a set job such as next file, copy, move or delete.
trash
The trash is a hidden folder named .trash where deleted local files wait, so you can still bring them back with Undo before the resource is closed.
TV
A TV is a television, an Android TV box or a media box that either runs the app with a remote control or shows what you cast to it.
video player
The video player is the player screen when it plays a video, with controls for pausing, jumping forward or back and changing the sound.
virtual resource
A virtual resource is a ready-made resource that gathers files of one kind from all over this device into one list, such as All Music or Camera Photos, without you picking a folder.
watch
The watch is a Wear OS smartwatch paired with your phone, which can browse, play and record files with the FastMedia Wear app.
Wear Companion
Wear Companion is the program on the phone that connects to your watch and decides which resources, streams and settings the phone shares with it.
welcome wizard
The welcome wizard is the series of pages Fast Media Sorter & Organizer shows on first launch - language and theme, device profile, network sources, capabilities, permissions and default player - before you reach the resource list; you can open it again from Settings.
widget
A widget is a small live block from Fast Media Sorter & Organizer that you place on the Android home screen, such as Favorites, Random Photo Frame, Stopwatch or Scheduled Operations.
