Fast Media Sorter - Product Overview

Fast Media Sorter is a high-performance, privacy-first multimedia organizer, universal media player, and desktop environment designed for Android phones, tablets, TVs, Wear OS smartwatches, and OpenXR VR headsets.

📖 Browse All 22 Categories 🔤 A-Z Subject Index 📥 PDF Offline Handbook

What is Fast Media Sorter?

Traditional mobile gallery and file manager apps are built for casual viewing. When dealing with thousands of photos, gigabytes of lossless music, sprawling video libraries, network storage (NAS), and cloud drives, standard tools become sluggish and cumbersome.

Fast Media Sorter is engineered from the ground up to solve this:

Lightning Fast

One-Tap Sorting

Sort, move, and copy files to predefined targets with a single tap, gesture, or keyboard shortcut.

Universal Playback

Any Format & Source

Seamless playback for FLAC, MKV, MP4, PDF, EPUB, M3U streams, and cloud/network storage without local copying.

Zero Telemetry

100% Privacy & Offline

No analytics trackers, no account required, all sorting and statistics remain strictly on your local device.

Ecosystem

Watch & VR Companion

Sync playlists, control playback from your wrist, or view 3D/180° spatial cinema in OpenXR VR headsets.

The Seven Editions (Flavors)

To comply with different distribution channels (Google Play, F-Droid, Meta Horizon Store, legacy Android devices) while providing maximal capabilities, Fast Media Sorter is published in 7 distinct editions:

Standard

Standard Edition

The flagship build for Google Play. Full feature set including media player, sorting, cloud integration, OCR, desktop launcher, and Wear companion.

FOSS

F-Droid / FOSS Edition

100% libre software with zero proprietary binary dependencies (no Google Drive / Play Services). Fully reproducible builds.

Lite

Lite Edition

Ultra-compact footprint for resource-constrained devices, focusing strictly on core file management, browsing, and sorting.

Photos

Photos Edition

Specialized for digital photo frames and photography enthusiasts. Features advanced slideshows, EXIF tools, and gallery curation.

Legacy

Legacy Edition

Backwards-compatible edition optimized for older Android versions (API 21+) and vintage media tablets.

VR

VR Edition

Standalone OpenXR package built for Meta Quest headsets with spatial immersive cinema and hands-free controls.

NoLegal

Direct / NoLegal Edition

Direct APK distribution with unrestricted local and network stream codecs, SFTP/SMB servers, and power-user utilities.

Quick Start Guide in 3 Steps

Step 1: Welcome Setup Wizard

On initial launch, Fast Media Sorter detects your device hardware (smartphone, TV box, tablet, or car unit) and recommends an optimized preset. Choose your preferred language, theme, and grant storage permissions.

Step-by-step Setup Walkthrough →

Step 2: Add Sources & Destination Targets

Select which local folders, SD cards, SMB network shares, or cloud storage accounts to monitor. Configure your Quick Sort destination buttons (e.g. Favorites, Work, Archive, To Review).

Configuring Sources & Destinations →

Step 3: Browse, Play & One-Tap Sort

Swipe through media with full gesture controls. When organizing, tap a destination icon or press a remapped key to instantly move or copy the current item and advance to the next file.
