# Phase 04 - honest-video-digital-zoom

**Goal:** Digital zoom beyond the CameraX native max is reachable AND baked into the recorded video, matching the preview (owner Q1 = keep extended range + honestly crop video).

## Context

- Today `setZoomRatio(ratio)` clamps optical/native at `capabilities.maxZoomRatio`; beyond it `digitalZoomFactor` scales the `PreviewView` (`scaleX/scaleY`, a visual crop) and, for photo only, centre-crops the JPEG. Video never receives the digital crop -> the recorded file is wider than the preview (spec §1, Goal 6).
- Native CameraX zoom (`setZoomRatio` within `maxZoomRatio`) already reaches preview + photo + video uniformly via the HAL `SCALER_CROP_REGION`; only the app-invented digital range beyond that is the problem.
- **Chosen approach: B (post-record Media3 Transformer)** - locked from the verification brief (workflow `wf_a152d8a6-70c`). Record natively, then re-encode the MP4 with a centred crop+scale via `androidx.media3:media3-transformer:1.2.1` (new dep; `media3-effect` already present) after `Finalize`; keep the existing `scaleX/scaleY` preview crop for the live viewfinder. Reuses the async post-capture crop model already used for JPEGs.
  - **Why B over A (live CameraEffect/SurfaceProcessor):** B is deterministic, testable, and dodges the real-time GLES + `PREVIEW|VIDEO_CAPTURE` stream-sharing device-compat minefield (OEM/GPU breakage, thermal throttling, dropped frames). A is architecturally ideal (single pass, zero generational loss, preview==file, mid-record zoom) but carries the highest device risk and owns EGL/thread/`SurfaceOutput` lifecycle - deferred unless B's re-encode cost proves unacceptable on device.
  - **B implementation notes (from brief):** `androidx.media3.effect.Crop(left,right,bottom,top)` in NDC (e.g. `Crop(-0.5f,0.5f,-0.5f,0.5f)` for 2x centre crop) + `Presentation` to upscale back to output size; consecutive matrix ops fuse into one GL pass. NO transmux fast-path (frames change) - budget a full re-encode: background job, progress state, temp-file cleanup. Preserve rotation/duration/metadata.
