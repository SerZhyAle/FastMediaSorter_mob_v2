Wear OS release.

• Voice notes on the watch: record, listen, rename, send to the phone.
• Audio keeps playing after you leave the app; the home screen shows what is sounding.
• The image viewer opens on the picture - tap the sides to page, the middle for controls.
• The channel you played last sits on the home screen.
• An edit or deletion of a network source now reaches the other device.
• Lists reopen where you left them.
