💡 Usage Scenarios

🖼️

Digital Photo Frame

Turn any Android tablet into an always-on digital photo frame. Stream photos from home PC (SMB) or cloud storage with zero local space used. Set slide intervals, add background music and enjoy.

Guide
🚗

In-Car Music

Install FMS on your Android car stereo. The built-in «All Music» resource gives immediate access to your collection, and steering wheel buttons integrate via background audio services.

Guide
🍿

Home Cinema & VR

Watch series from PC or cloud directly on your phone and VR headset (Quest/Pico). Auto-advance starts the next episode automatically, and VR edition opens a giant cinema screen.

Guide
🧹

Download Organizer

Sort files on your device or remote server. Set up custom destination buttons and organize files into their proper target folders with a single tap.

Guide
📷

Scheduled Camera Backup

Automatically copy new camera photos and videos from your phone to a home computer or NAS over Wi-Fi (SMB) during night charging or at any set interval.

Guide
📡

SMB Network Setup

Step-by-step guide for sharing folders on Windows/macOS/Linux and connecting to them instantly from your mobile devices in a single Wi-Fi network.

Guide
