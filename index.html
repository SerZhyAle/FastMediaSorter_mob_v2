FastMediaSorter v2

Smart Media Management and Playback for Android & VR
Release v2.x • Open Source

A free Android app that sorts and plays photos, videos, audio and documents from local storage, SMB/NAS, SFTP/FTP and cloud - for anyone who organizes media on phones, tablets, TV boxes, car head units or VR headsets.

Get it on Google Play GitHub Releases APK
  1. Install the APK for your device (or get it on Google Play).
  2. Allow installs from this source if Android asks.
  3. Add a folder, network share or cloud account - then open and sort.

Duplicate Cleanup

Standard / VR

Find identical files across storage before a photo archive or download folder grows out of control.

Details
