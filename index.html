FastMediaSorter v2

Professional Media Organization for Android
Version 2.x • Open Source

✨ Key Features

  • Local storage management
  • SMB/CIFS network shares support
  • SFTP & FTP protocol support
  • Cloud storage support (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
  • EPUB E-Book Reader support
  • Advanced video player with ExoPlayer
  • High-performance image viewer (Coil)
  • Batch file operations (copy/move/delete)
  • Favorites system
  • Slideshow mode with configurable intervals
  • Slideshow Background Music
  • Audio Player with Photo Background
  • Binary Files Support (ZIP, APK, thumbnails)
  • Keyboard & Mouse Support
  • Lyrics Support
  • Image Editing (Filters, adjustments)
  • Hidden Files Support
  • Thumbnail caching (2GB default, up to 16GB)
  • Pagination for large folders (1000+ files)
  • Auto-Translation (OCR) for Images/PDFs
  • Widget Support for HomeScreen
  • Clean Architecture + MVVM pattern
  • Advanced Touch Gestures

💡 Usage Scenarios

🖼️

Digital Photo Frame

Give a second life to an old tablet! Connect it to your home PC or cloud, start a slideshow with background music, and enjoy memories without copying files.

🍿

Home Cinema & VR

Watch series from PC or cloud directly on your phone or VR headset. No copying needed - just press play, and the next episode starts automatically.

🧹

Download Organizer

Sort clutter on your device or remote computer. Set up destination buttons and organize files into folders with a single tap.
